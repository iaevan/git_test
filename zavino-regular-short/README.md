# regular. — a zavino. labs teaser (animated short)

The fourth film in the series, made with the **Shorts Animator** skill in the same **clean** look as *Meet Pixel* — but for a product that is **not built yet**. It says so: *"So we're building something at Zavino Labs"*, a *Work in progress* tag, a *Concept preview* label on every product screen, and a *Coming soon* end card.

**The product (working name `regular.`)** — smart review + customer tracking for restaurants and cafés: a QR code on every table earns a verified phone number (OTP), the order and the bill; then a private rating + Google review link, instant low-rating alerts, regulars / big spenders / lapsed customers, AI-written campaigns the owner approves (Eid, Ramadan, Pohela Boishakh), offer rules, and a monthly report. See [`IDEATION.md`](IDEATION.md) for positioning, naming, the concepts considered and the backlog.

**The story (≈87 s, 9:16, 37 shots)** — one idea: **growing shouldn't mean forgetting.** When Nila opened *Café 27* she knew every customer — Hasan, Sumi, Arif — by name and by order. Then it got busy, and somewhere in the rush she stopped knowing them. A coffee came out cold, nobody noticed, the customer just left, and a week later one star told her what nobody had. So zavino. labs is building a way for cafés to remember: a QR on the table, **Rafi** scans, checks in, picks his order; one code, one bill — and he isn't a stranger anymore. An hour later, *how was it?* — happy → Google; not happy → Nila hears about it first, in time to make it right (she brings Tania a fresh coffee). Visit by visit Rafi becomes a regular; one morning Nila says **"The usual, Rafi?"** — *"Oh! You remembered!"* Then the owner's view (who's coming back, who's slipping away; Eid and Pohela Boishakh outreach, only when she chooses) → **regular. Every customer, remembered. Coming soon from Zavino Labs.**

The narration is *directed*: Eleven v4 audio tags (`[warmly, nostalgic]`, `[softly, sadly]`, `[hopeful, brightening]`…) at stability 0, with three voices — narrator **Julian**, **Nila** (Maeve) and **Rafi** (Dylan) in the payoff line. Voice levels keep their natural quiet/lively contrast instead of being peak-normalised.

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 tools/make_short.py                  # short.json (script, shots, cues)
python3 tools/voice.py                       # download, slow, space and time the 16 narration clips → voice/NN.wav + marks.json
python3 tools/make_short.py                  # again: cue times scale to the real shot lengths
tools/build.sh                               # scenes.js
export CHROME=/path/to/chrome-headless-shell SHORTS_GL=swiftshader   # CPU painting in a container
node skill/scripts/animate.mjs frames .      # one frame per shot → frames/sheet.jpg
node skill/scripts/animate.mjs render .      # the MP4 (~18 min on 4 CPU cores in clean mode)
```

## Patches to the skill (in `skill/`)

* `scripts/lib/chrome.mjs` — `--no-sandbox --disable-dev-shm-usage --enable-unsafe-swiftshader` so headless Chrome runs as root in a container, painting in software.
* `scripts/runtime/stage.js` — `look.mode: "clean"` skips the brush pass; captions are rounded pills.
* `scripts/animate.mjs` — `voice.clips` (one pre-trimmed audio file per line), `voice.marks` (per caption chunk, when it is spoken — cuts land on the words instead of a character-count guess), per-line `pause`, `cues` (sound effects placed by shot), and **web fonts**: any `assets/*.woff2` is registered as `@font-face` and loaded before the first drawing (used for Manrope).
* `scripts/animate.mjs audio <dir>` — rebuilds only the soundtrack (voice, music, cues) and muxes it into the latest render, video untouched (use when only a voice clip changed and its length is the same).
* `scripts/animate.mjs patch <dir> <shot>` — re-paints one shot and splices it into the latest `renders/vN.mp4` (about 1 minute), for small fixes without a full re-render.
